Overview & Data Handling

Critter Kart GP is a free arcade kart racing game with no advertisements and no real-money purchases. Unlocked karts and cup records are stored locally on your device. Optional online leaderboards only transmit an anonymous deviceId, chosen nickname, and lap/cup score.

Advertisements & Third-Party Trackers

Critter Kart GP contains no advertisements, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics trackers.

Data Deletion & Contact

To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear app storage on your device. For any privacy questions or leaderboard removal requests, contact natanhae@gmail.com or visit github.com/VaelNTN/critter-kart-gp.
