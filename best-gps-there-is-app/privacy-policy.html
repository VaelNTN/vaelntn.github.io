Overview & Data Handling

Road Mapper uses your GPS coordinates locally on your device to display your map position, provide turn-by-turn voice guidance, and request routes from your selected routing provider (OpenStreetMap/Valhalla/Photon). Vehicle dimensions, favorites, and settings are stored strictly locally. Zero ads and zero analytics trackers.

Advertisements & Third-Party Trackers

Road Mapper contains no advertisements, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics trackers.

Data Deletion & Contact

To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear app storage on your device. For any privacy questions or leaderboard removal requests, contact natanhae@gmail.com or visit github.com/VaelNTN/best-gps-there-is-app.
