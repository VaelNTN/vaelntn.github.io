Overview & Data Handling

Dog Climber is a free arcade climbing game with no real-money purchases, no advertisements, and no third-party analytics SDKs. Game progress and nicknames are stored locally. Optional online highscores only send an anonymous deviceId, chosen nickname, and numeric score.

Advertisements & Third-Party Trackers

Dog Climber contains no advertisements, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics trackers.

Data Deletion & Contact

To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear app storage on your device. For any privacy questions or leaderboard removal requests, contact natanhae@gmail.com or visit github.com/VaelNTN/dog-climber.
