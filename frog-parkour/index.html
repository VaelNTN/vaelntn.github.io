Overview & Data Handling

Frog Parkour is a free arcade platformer game with no advertisements and no real-money purchases. Game progress and highscores are stored locally on your device. Optional online leaderboards only transmit an anonymous deviceId, chosen nickname, and numeric score.

Advertisements & Third-Party Trackers

Frog Parkour contains no advertisements, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics trackers.

Data Deletion & Contact

To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear app storage on your device. For any privacy questions or leaderboard removal requests, contact natanhae@gmail.com or visit github.com/VaelNTN/frog-parkour.
