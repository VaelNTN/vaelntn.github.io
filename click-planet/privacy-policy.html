Privacy Policy — Click Planet

Last updated: 2026-11-24
Developer: VaelNTN Studios
Package ID: com.clickplanet.game

Overview & Data Handling

Click Planet is a free sci-fi idle clicker game with no advertisements and no real-money purchases. Game progress and upgrades are stored locally on your device. Optional online leaderboards only transmit an anonymous deviceId, chosen nickname, and score.

Advertisements & Third-Party Trackers

Click Planet contains no advertisements, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics trackers.

Data Deletion & Contact

To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear app storage on your device. For any privacy questions or leaderboard removal requests, contact natanhae@gmail.com or visit github.com/VaelNTN/click-planet.
